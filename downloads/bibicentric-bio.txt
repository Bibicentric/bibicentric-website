BIBICENTRIC / BIBI
Nigerian R&B and soul singer, songwriter and performer
Lagos-born; based in the Washington, DC, Maryland and Virginia area.

Bola Akinlaja, known professionally as BIBI and Bibicentric, is a Nigerian R&B and soul singer, songwriter and performer. She began music training at 15 at Tenstrings Music Institute and released the singles Jeje and Endlessly as Bibi in 2016. Under the name Bibicentric, she released Magic and her debut EP, Monophobia, in 2020. Her music draws on soul, R&B and jazz, with songs exploring love, family and emotional honesty. Her performance archive includes acoustic sessions, full-band live sets and orchestra-backed performances.
